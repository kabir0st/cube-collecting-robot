For LEGO gears, the rolling diameter in millimeters matches the tooth count:

Z40 → 40 mm rolling diameter
Z24 → 24 mm rolling diameter

The wheel part 3482 is the classic small LEGO wheel used with tires around the 30.4 × 14 size family, giving an overall rolling diameter close to 30 mm.

You can also calculate gear ratios:

40T driving 24T → ratio = 1.67:1
24T driving 40T → ratio = 0.6:1

And the wheel circumference (for ~30.4 mm diameter) is approximately:

C=πd≈π×30.4≈95.5 mm

So the wheel rolls about 95.5 mm per full rotation.
